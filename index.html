Why design engineering?

I believe the future of design goes beyond static screens in Figma. That's why I'm moving from product design toward design engineering: designing interfaces and building them in code.

It started with a small experiment: this portfolio. I used it to learn new tools like Git and GitHub, but mainly to discover how I can keep my judgment at the center of the process while speeding up the execution with AI.
