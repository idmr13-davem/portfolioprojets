About me

I’m David, a product designer from Colombia with a background in graphic design. I began my career in marketing agencies, working across branding, advertising, and visual design before transitioning into UX.

My first UX role was as a web designer, back when the discipline was still taking shape. Since then, I’ve spent eight years designing digital products across marketplaces, fintech, and B2B services. I’ve worked in startups and established companies, including nearly five years at Mercado Libre, the biggest tech company in Latam.

That range of experience has shaped how I approach product design: combining visual craft, business thinking, and a deep understanding of context. You can find the full timeline in my CV.
