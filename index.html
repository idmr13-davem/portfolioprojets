Case studies

Mercado Libre sign-up

Role: Senior UX analyst / UI designer

I led the iteration of Mercado Libre’s sign-up model, impacting 60 million users across 9 Latin American countries.

Outcome

I reduced average sign-up time from 3:25 minutes to 52 seconds and increased conversion by more than 10%, depending on the country.

See case study

Motion design system - Motion Level

Role: Senior UX analyst / UI designer

I co-led the first phase of Mercado Libre’s motion design system, developing the conceptual foundation for the Motion Level framework.

Outcome

I created the conceptual framework that unifies how expressive motion is at Mercado Libre, impacting more than 200 UX analysts and developers within the organization.

See case study

Sign-up for minors in Mexico

Role: Senior UX analyst / UI designer

I designed Mercado Pago’s sign-up flow for minors in Mexico, working within Mexican regulations, among the most demanding in Latin America.

Outcome

I iterated on the Mexico flow using insights from Argentina and Brazil, reducing identity verification time from 24 hours to 20 seconds.

Switching accounts across countries

Role: Senior UX analyst / UI designer

I created the cross-country account-switching experience, an animated flow that people with accounts in several Latin American countries see when switching between them.

Outcome

I improved the experience for 200k people with multiple accounts across Brazil, Argentina and Uruguay, a number that has continued to grow in recent quarters.
