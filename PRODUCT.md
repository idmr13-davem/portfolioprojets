## Users

Recruiters assess fit quickly; hiring managers examine decisions and evidence; design leaders examine leadership and systems. These audiences and their needs are supplied in the project's AGENTS.md instructions.

## Product Purpose

David Montealegre's personal portfolio presents his product design work to technology and automotive employers. Visitors explore case studies, learn about David, read articles, and contact him.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript remain the implementation. English is the default with Spanish available. Preserve authorized facts; do not invent metrics or present production results for the active Accounts for minors in Mexico project. Publication scope (October 14, 2026): Registro ML, Motion Level and Accounts for minors in Mexico are public; Switching accounts across countries stays protected and is excluded from releases until server authentication and authorization exist. V2 was published on October 15, 2026. The local Home compositions in `variants/` are exploration only and are not part of the published site.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly requires preserving the existing design system: canonical CSS tokens, Inter typography, palette, spacing, radii, and shared controls. Composition may vary between the three local versions.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved Home content in index.html and js/i18n.js; canonical tokens in css/tokens/figma.css; shared controls in css/components.css; existing case-study, portrait, and article assets. These supply evidence rather than invented illustrations or claims.

## Product Principles

Progressive disclosure; demonstrate craft in the site; show ownership through decisions and outcomes; prioritize impact metrics; motion serves comprehension.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences. Animate transform and opacity only; page transitions cannot exceed 500 ms. Preserve readable responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, and visible focus.
