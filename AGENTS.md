- Portfolio analytics research status as of 2026-08-13: visitor analytics are feasible on the current GitHub Pages deployment by adding a client-side analytics snippet; GitHub repository Traffic is not a substitute for website analytics. The leading recommendation to evaluate next session is Cloudflare Web Analytics because it is free, privacy-first, works without moving the current GoDaddy/GitHub Pages DNS, and reports page views, top pages, countries, referrers, devices, and performance without cookies or individual visitor profiles. Its current limitations are no UTM campaign reporting and no custom event tracking. If David wants campaign attribution for CV, LinkedIn, email, or social links and conversion events such as contact clicks or CV downloads, compare Plausible (simpler, privacy-focused, paid, supports UTMs and custom goals) with GA4 (free and more granular, but cookie/consent and privacy implementation are more involved). Before implementation, choose the desired measurement depth and then add an appropriate privacy disclosure and consent behavior where required. Never attempt to identify named individuals; location analytics are approximate and aggregated.
